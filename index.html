TRACE

Every rep counts.

A workout tracker for people who train consistently

Log your sets, see your records, follow your consistency, and train with friends. Most of what you record stays on your phone.

What it does

  • Fast workout logging with a rest timer
  • History, personal records and analytics
  • Weekly consistency and a monthly season with friends and groups
  • Lock screen and Apple Watch support (iPhone)
  • Works offline. An account is optional.

Status

TRACE is in private testing. It is not in the app stores yet.
